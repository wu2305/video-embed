## Rules

- `text` on `bg`, `panel` and `field`. `muted` for labels, metadata and ghost buttons (at least 5.8:1 on every surface).
- `accent` is the single interactive colour: the primary button, the focus ring, a selected card, a named person.
- `good` means *matched* (scene range, score, saved). `warn` means *look twice* (copy, merge, unnamed). `bad` means *blocked* (error, no such person).
- Every signal colour sits next to a word or number. Green and red are close in lightness, so hue alone never carries meaning.
- `line` is a separator (about 1.3:1). Selection and focus always add `accent` on top of it.
