# Unified Search component gallery

Components for the Unified Search Gateway's console and the scenes in `STORYBOOK.md` at the repo root.
The gateway searches the Archive System, NAS documents (the `mmindex` index) and a BI platform's metrics in one
query, filters every result to what the signed-in user may open, and is updated nightly
(`docs/UNIFIED_SEARCH_DESIGN.md`). The data is mocked, so no index, gateway or backend is needed.

Image, video and audio search is hidden: the customer does not search media. Those components (faces, notes,
clips, the People and Clip pages) stay in `src/` and in `npm test`, tagged `!dev` so the sidebar leaves them out.

## How the gallery is organised

Atomic design, smallest first. Start at **Pages/Overview** to see every page at a glance, and **Foundations**
to build.

Every story renders in a dark and a light theme. Switch with the **Theme** toolbar, or pick **Side by side**.

| Level | Stories | Built from |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundations** | Colour, Type, Space and shape | the tokens at the top of `styles.css` |
| **Atoms** | `Button`, `Tag`, `Label`, `Chip`, `Kbd`, `Panel` | tokens only |
| **Molecules** | `Field`, `ResultCard`, `ArchiveCard`, `MetricCard`, `Freshness`, `MetaList` | atoms |
| **Organisms** | `TopBar`, `SearchBar`, `ResultGrid`, `PreviewPanel`, `MetricPreview` | molecules and atoms |
| **Templates** | `AppShell` | top bar, page header, body slot |
| **Pages** | `Overview` (live previews), `Search` | organisms in the shell, with mock state |
| **Scenes** | scenes 6 and 7 of `STORYBOOK.md`, which no page shows | organisms |

Every story is also a test: `npm test` runs each one in Chromium, once per theme, including its `play`
steps and an accessibility check that fails on any axe violation (colour contrast included).

Component stories hold the states and the interaction tests; the pages show each scene's flow.

| Scene | Where to see it | Status in the repo |

Scenes 1–3 (name a face, write on the video, who is doing what) are media scenes and are hidden.

and `IndexInfo` are all proposals: the Archive vendor's response shape is still to be confirmed.
